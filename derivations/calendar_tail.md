# Calendar no-arbitrage for ρ < 0: the far right wing (route 3)

Epic 0016, started 2026-10-28. Route 1 (`calendar_derivative.md`) reduced the conjecture to
signing ∂C/∂u and found that the ratio (T1+T2)/T1 decays towards zero in the far right wing,
with many cells underflowing double precision. This note settles the sign there to leading
Laplace order and proves the one ingredient that could have reversed it.
**Outcome: a lemma (proved) and a leading-order tail result (not yet a proof).**

Notation as in `calendar_derivative.md`: F = D = 1, u = √(τQ(τ)), s² = ln(1+k²),
ζ = e^{sZ − s²/2}, y = u²ζ, M = e^{ρ√y Z − ρ²y/2}, m(u) = E[M], A = M/m, w = (1−ρ²)y.

## Lemma (normaliser). For ρ < 0, m(u) is strictly decreasing in u, and

    m(u) = E_V[ 1 / (1 + W(β e^{sV/2})) ],   V ~ N(0,1),   β = (s/2)|ρ| u e^{−s²/4},

with W the principal Lambert function; m ≡ 1 at ρ = 0.

*Proof.* With c = ρu e^{−s²/4} < 0 and g(Z) = e^{sZ/2}, the exponent of M is c Z g − c²g²/2,
so φ(Z)M(Z) = φ(Z − c g(Z)): the tilted density is a standard normal density in
V = Z − c g(Z) = Z + |c| g(Z). This map is a strictly increasing bijection of ℝ with
dV/dZ = 1 + (s/2)|c| g(Z) = 1 + (s/2)(V − Z). Writing t = V − Z, t e^{st/2} = |c| e^{sV/2},
so (s/2)t = W(β e^{sV/2}). Hence m = ∫ φ(V) dV / (1 + W(β e^{sV/2})). For fixed V the
integrand decreases strictly in β, and β is proportional to u. ∎

Checked in `test_calendar_tail.py` (symbolic completion of the square and Jacobian; the
Lambert form against direct quadrature of E[M] on 40 random (u, k, ρ); m' < 0 by finite
differences; m = 1 at ρ = 0). Side result: under the share measure P* the level factor is
Z = V − (2/s) W(β e^{sV/2}) with V having density φ(V) / (m (1 + W(β e^{sV/2}))), which is
the natural starting point for route 2.

## Far-wing sign to leading order

With L = ln K and B = L + ln m + y/2 − ρ√y Z, P(S > K) = E_Z[N(−B/√w)]. Let Z* maximise
Ψ = −Z²/2 − B²/(2(1−ρ²)y). Using the saddle condition to eliminate ∂Ψ/∂y, the envelope
theorem gives, to leading Laplace order,

    ∂ ln P(S > K)/∂u ≈ G = (2/(s u)) (Z* − ρB*/((1−ρ²)√y*)) − B* (m'/m)/((1−ρ²) y*).

For ρ < 0, Z* > 0 and B* > 0 (both hold in the far wing) the first term is positive, and by
the lemma so is the second. Since C(K) = ∫_K^∞ P(S > x) dx, a positive ∂_u P(S > x) for all
x ≥ K gives ∂C/∂u > 0. Asymptotically, Z* ≈ ln(2L/u²)/s and G ≈ (2/(su))(Z* + |ρ|√(2L)/(1−ρ²)):
the tail probability gains with u at rate √L, which is why the calendar increment stays
positive although T2 cancels T1 to leading order.

- dC/du is positive at all 144 points (ρ ∈ {−0.05, −0.49, −0.9, −0.99}, k ∈ {0.1, 2.2, 3},
  u ∈ {0.3, 2}, ln K/F ∈ {0.5, 2, 10, 40, 200, 1000}), including every cell that
  underflowed in route 1; the smallest ratio dC/du / T1 is 0.026 (ρ = −0.99, k = 3, u = 0.3,
  ln K/F = 1000). Where both tables cover a point they agree to the printed digits
  (e.g. 0.7327, 0.3327, 0.1192, 0.1174), from different code.
- G matches ∂ ln P/∂u to within 3% for ln K/F ≥ 10 (within 0.2% at 200 and 1000).
- m'/m ranges from −0.31 to −0.0025 here, negative as the lemma requires.

## What is still missing for a proof

1. Control of the Laplace remainder, so that ∂_u P(S > x) > 0 holds for all x ≥ K₀(ρ, k, u)
   with an explicit K₀, uniformly over compact parameter sets (and as k → 0, where Z* grows
   like 1/s).
2. The finite-strike region x < ln K₀, where T2 is unsigned (route 1). Options: a certified
   (interval-arithmetic) check on a parameter box, or route 2 using the P* representation
   above.
3. The limits u → 0 and u → ∞, and ρ → −1.
