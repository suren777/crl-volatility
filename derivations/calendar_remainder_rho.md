# Remainder for rho < 0 (epic 0016, task 05-remainder-rho)

Notation as in `calendar_remainder.md`: r = |rho|, lambda = L' = L + ln m > 0, sigma = s^2,
mu = sqrt(2 lambda), c0 = ln(2 lambda/u^2) + sigma/2, v = ln y - ln(2 lambda), so y = 2 lambda e^v,
Z = (v + c0)/s, A = E_nu e^v E_nu e^{-v}. Labels: PROVED (argument written out below),
VERIFIED NUMERICALLY (`calendar_remainder_rho.py`, `test_calendar_remainder_rho.py`), ASYMPTOTIC
(formal, from `calendar_laplace.md`), CONJECTURED (stated, not established).

## Result

No inequality R'(L) < 1 for rho < 0 is proved here. Goal (A) is not delivered. Goal (B) is
delivered only as the exact reduction below and the missing inequalities, stated precisely.

- PROVED: the exact potential V(v) of nu at r > 0, the covariance form of R', and the curvature
  formula for V''. The rho = 0 Brascamp-Lieb argument does not transfer directly, because V'' is
  not bounded below by P_r = 1/sigma + lambda/(2(1 - r^2)) on the support (see "Obstruction").
- VERIFIED NUMERICALLY: R' < 1 at all 540 grid points with sigma <= 2, rho in [-0.99, -0.05],
  u in {0.1, 1, 5}, k in {0.05, 0.3, 1, 2, 2.5}, L' in {0.5, ..., 3000}. The minimum of 1 - R' is
  about 1.3e-3. The shipped-grid problem of `Model.r_slope` is avoided by a widened z-grid.
- ASYMPTOTIC: 1 - R' ~ 2r/(s mu) = sqrt(2) r/(s sqrt(L')). The scan supports this when r mu is
  large: at r = 0.99 and L' = 3000 the ratio (1 - R')/(2r/(s mu)) lies in [0.897, 1.001] over the
  sampled (u, k). At small r the ratio is large, because the rho = 0 margin dominates. The
  crossover is at r mu of order 1.
- CONJECTURED: for every delta > 0 and s^2 <= 2, R'(L) < 1 for rho in [-1 + delta, 0) and L'
  above an explicit L0(k, delta) uniform in u. The scan supports this but does not prove it.

## Setup (PROVED; sympy: `test_exponent_equals_minus_potential_symbolic`)

With rho = -r, the conditional density of Z is proportional to phi(Z) phi(d2)/sqrt(y). Expanding
the exponent, and using (y/2 + lambda)/sqrt(y) = mu cosh(v/2) and y/4 + lambda + lambda^2/y =
lambda(1 + cosh v) = mu^2 cosh^2(v/2), gives exactly

    -(log density of nu in v) = V(v) + const,

    V(v) = (v + c0)^2/(2 sigma) + v/2 + q^2/(2(1 - r^2)),   q = mu cosh(v/2) + r (v + c0)/s.

The identity lambda(1 + cosh v) = mu^2 cosh^2(v/2) is exact, which is what makes the cross terms
combine into q^2.

## Covariance form of R' (PROVED)

R' = Cov_nu(y/2, d2/sqrt(w)), and d2/sqrt(w) = -(1/(1 - r^2)) [1/2 + e^{-v}/2 + r Z/sqrt(y)]. So

    R'(1 - r^2) = (lambda/2)(A - 1) - lambda r Cov_nu(e^v, Z/sqrt(y)),
    Z/sqrt(y) = (v + c0) e^{-v/2}/(s mu).

Equivalently R'(1 - r^2) = (lambda/2)(E y E(1/y) - 1) - (r/2) Cov(y, Z/sqrt y), the split form of
`calendar_band.md`. At r = 0 this reduces to the rho = 0 identity of `calendar_remainder.md`.
Against `Model.r_slope` on a widened grid the covariance form agrees to 2.2e-5 over the scan.

## Curvature (PROVED; sympy: `test_curvature_formula_symbolic`)

With q' = mu sinh(v/2)/2 + r/s and q'' = mu cosh(v/2)/4,

    V'' = 1/sigma + (q'^2 + q q'')/(1 - r^2).

At r = 0 this is 1/sigma + (lambda/2) cosh v >= P. For r > 0 the term q q'' is negative on
{q < 0}, which is the set r (v + c0)/s < -mu cosh(v/2).

## Obstruction (PROVED for the set; VERIFIED NUMERICALLY for its mass)

On {q < 0} the lower bound V'' >= P_r is not available, so P_r cannot be used as a global lower
bound on the support. Two facts are checked on the scan:

- The nu-mass of {q < 0} is not uniformly negligible at small and moderate lambda: the maximum is
  0.50 at lambda = 0.5, 0.42 at lambda = 2, 0.26 at lambda = 10 (e.g. u = 5, k = 0.05, r = 0.99),
  and 1.5e-4 at lambda = 50. It is zero at lambda = 300 and 3000.
- min over the support of V''/P_r is about -1.87 on the scan, so V'' can be negative there.

Therefore the Brascamp-Lieb step of `calendar_remainder.md` (Step 3) does not apply directly to
the tilts. A tilted-family argument would need an explicit tail bound on {q < 0} and a convexity
replacement on that set.

## Missing inequalities (for goal (A); not proved)

1. An explicit bound nu({q < 0}) <= eps(lambda, delta, k), with eps small enough that the
   remainder below is dominated by the margin, uniformly in u.
2. A replacement for Brascamp-Lieb on the tilts that is valid on {q >= 0} and controlled on
   {q < 0}, giving an explicit upper bound for A - 1 of the form
   (1 - r^2)(1 - r c0/(s mu)) + O(1/lambda), with explicit constants.
3. An explicit bound for the cross term lambda r Cov(e^v, (v + c0) e^{-v/2})/(s mu) of the form
   (2r/(s mu))(1 - c0/2) + O(1/lambda), uniform in c0. The c0 terms cancel only at leading order,
   and the cancellation must be made explicit with an O(1/lambda) remainder.
4. The crossover at r mu of order 1 needs an explicit Lipschitz bound in r from the rho = 0 bound,
   which is not derived here.

## Decisions

- No inequality is claimed as PROVED for rho < 0. The scan is VERIFIED NUMERICALLY only and is
  printed by `calendar_remainder_rho.py` (the scan section below is generated by that script).
- The tests check identities symbolically and at sample points. No bound is claimed for rho < 0,
  so the "bound >= truth" hypothesis test does not apply.
- The shipped `Model.r_slope` z-grid is not changed (outside scope); `wide_model` here widens it
  for rho < 0 in this module only.
